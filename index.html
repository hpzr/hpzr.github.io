01 / Introduction

About

I am a Deaf linguist working across sign language research, language documentation, and community engagement. My background in classical philology informs how I think about language, evidence, and the histories of knowledge.

At Tallinn University, my work with the Estonian Sign Language Research Group (EVKUR) brings linguistic inquiry into conversation with the experiences and priorities of Deaf communities. I also work across European collaborations concerned with sign language education and language resources.
